Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our laundry and dry cleaning process.

How does doorstep pickup and delivery work?

When you book online, our valet arrives at your designated time slot with a hygienic laundry bag. We verify your items, tag them, and return them cleaned, pressed, and hung at your doorstep within 24 hours.

Will I receive an order receipt and confirmation in my Gmail?

Yes! As soon as you submit your booking, an automated order confirmation slip is emailed directly to your specified Gmail with your unique Order ID, scheduled pickup slot, address, and itemized bill.

What if I need urgent laundry within hours?

We offer an Express 6-Hour Delivery option for a nominal surcharge of ₹99. Just tick the "Express 6-Hour Delivery" checkbox in your cart before booking.

How do you care for delicate items and designer garments?

All luxury wear, silks, woolens, and heavily embroidered items undergo individual fabric inspection and are cleaned using gentle hydrocarbon solvent dry cleaning methods without harsh agitation.
